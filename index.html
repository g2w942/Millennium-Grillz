10+ Years of Excellence

For over a decade, Millennium Grillz has been a cornerstone of dining in Nairobi CBD. Located opposite Jeevanjee Gardens along Moi Avenue, we have been serving fresh, delicious meals in a clean, warm, and homely environment.


Prepared by skilled chefs with a passion for quality, we welcome every guest like family. Whether you are craving our signature Nyama Choma, a quick fast-food bite, or refreshing juices, we promise great food, excellent service, and a comfortable place to relax.
